Research

I work on problems at the intersection of algorithmic game theory and AI safety. I am currently pursuing two research threads: (1) fair allocation under combinatorial constraints, and (2) strategyproof monitoring in multi-agent AI systems. Papers are listed below.

In Progress
Strategyproof Monitoring in Multi-Agent AI Systems
Nirdesh Bhandari
Work in progress, 2026

Formalizing multi-agent AI monitoring as a mechanism design problem, extending Qiu et al. (ICLR 2026) and Agarwal et al. (2026) to the multi-monitor setting. Applying peer prediction frameworks (Miller et al., Prelec, Witkowski and Parkes) to characterize when truthful safety reporting is incentive-compatible. Designing monitoring mechanisms with formal guarantees — strategyproofness and collusion resistance — for scalable AI oversight pipelines.

In Progress
Fair Allocation under Combinatorial Constraints
Nirdesh Bhandari, Arpita Biswas
Work in progress, 2026

Studying EF1 allocations maximizing Nash Social Welfare under item conflicts and shared resources. Modeling the "cost of sharing" for emergency power distribution across counties using real-world U.S. outage and social vulnerability data. Exploring incentive compatibility and strategyproofness in constrained settings.
